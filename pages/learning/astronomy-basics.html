Asteroids

Asteroids are small, rocky objects that orbit the Sun, primarily found in the asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter. Sometimes called minor planets, they are rocky, airless remnants left over from the early formation of our solar system about 4.6 billion years ago.

Comets

Comets are frozen leftovers from the formation of the solar system, composed of dust, rock, and ices that orbit the Sun.

Planets

Planets are celestial bodies that orbit stars, with our solar system's Sun being the primary star. These objects are characterized by their gravitational influence and the ability to reflect sunlight.

Satellites and Moons

An artificial body placed in orbit around the Earth, Moon, or another planet in order to collect information or for communication.
