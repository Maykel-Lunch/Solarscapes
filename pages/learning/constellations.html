✨ Constellations of the Night Sky

Orion - The Hunter

One of the most recognizable constellations in the night sky. Orion features bright stars Betelgeuse and Rigel, and the famous Orion Nebula. Visible in winter months in the Northern Hemisphere.

Ursa Major - Big Dipper

Contains the famous asterism known as the Big Dipper. These seven stars have helped navigators for centuries, particularly as a guide to finding Polaris, the North Star.

Cassiopeia - Queen

A distinctive W-shaped constellation named after the vain Queen Cassiopeia of Greek mythology. Easily visible in the Northern Sky and often used for navigation alongside the Big Dipper.

Pegasus - Winged Horse

Prominent autumn constellation featuring the Great Square of Pegasus. This asterism helps locate many other constellations and deep-sky objects in the northern autumn sky.

Scorpius - The Scorpion

A prominent zodiac constellation with a distinctive curved tail marked by bright stars. Home to the red supergiant star Antares, visible in the summer sky near the Milky Way.

Cygnus - The Swan

A magnificent summer constellation located along the Milky Way. Also known as the Northern Cross, it contains bright star Deneb and several deep-sky objects including the North America Nebula.
