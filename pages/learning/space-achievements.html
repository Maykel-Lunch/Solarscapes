🚀 Space Achievements

65/100
First Artificial Satellite (1957)

Sputnik 1 marked humanity's first step into space exploration. This Soviet satellite launched on October 4, 1957, opened the Space Age and revolutionized our understanding of space technology.

Moon Landing (1969)

Apollo 11 successfully landed astronauts Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin on the Moon. This monumental achievement demonstrated human capability in space exploration and remains one of the greatest accomplishments in history.

International Space Station (1998-Present)

The ISS represents humanity's continuous presence in space. Built through international cooperation, it serves as a laboratory for scientific research and stands as a symbol of peaceful space exploration and global collaboration.

Hubble Space Telescope (1990-Present)

Launched in 1990, the Hubble Space Telescope has revolutionized our view of the universe. Its discoveries about deep space, distant galaxies, and the age of the universe have fundamentally changed our cosmological understanding.
