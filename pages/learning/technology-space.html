🛰️ Technology and Space

75/100
Satellite Technology

Modern satellites enable communication, GPS, weather forecasting, and Earth observation. From communication satellites to weather monitoring, satellite technology has become essential to modern civilization and space exploration.

Rocket Propulsion Systems

Rocket propulsion is the cornerstone of space exploration. From chemical rockets to ion drives, propulsion technology determines our capability to reach space. Understanding thrust, fuel efficiency, and multi-stage rockets is crucial for space missions.

Space Telescopes

Space telescopes like Hubble and the James Webb Space Telescope observe the universe free from Earth's atmosphere. These instruments have revealed distant galaxies, exoplanets, and the earliest formations of the universe.

Robotics and Automation

Robotic rovers like Curiosity and Perseverance explore Mars, while robotic arms on the ISS perform critical tasks. Automation and AI are essential for exploring harsh environments where human presence is not yet feasible.
